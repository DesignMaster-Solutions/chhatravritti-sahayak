# Chhatravritti Sahayak — brief (Code for a Billion 2026, track Education)

Goal: a free, open-source agent that tells a student from a poor family in India which government
scholarships for Class 11 and above they can apply for this year, where (the National Scholarship
Portal or their state's portal), by when, with which documents, and the mistakes that get an
application rejected or the money stuck after approval, in English and Hindi, on a cheap phone.
Built for students, parents and the teachers or college clerks who fill these forms for them.

Why this one: on 05/11/2026 the hub has two real Education entries (issue #40, career guidance on
the Holistic Progress Card; issue #6, an accessible web view for learners) and one empty template
(#2). Nothing covers the money that keeps a poor student in school. "Dropout prevention" is named
in the Education area on codeforindia.org/hackathon. Timing: third-party sites report that the
2026-27 post-matric window on the National Scholarship Portal was extended to 30 November 2026
(VERIFY on scholarships.gov.in), so students are filling these forms right now.

## Verified facts (read in the official source on 05/11/2026; the ONLY facts the app may state)

G = https://scholarships.gov.in/public/schemeGuidelines  F = https://scholarships.gov.in/public/FAQ

Dates: `nsp-data.js` (generated by `tools/nsp_snapshot.py` from the three "Schemes on NSP" lists) holds, per
scheme and per state, the student closing date, defective-correction date, institute verification date and
state-level date, already read for 2026-27. These replace the secondary dates once in this brief. What the
list really says: most central schemes close 30-11-2026 (institute 15-12-2026), but PM YASASVI Top Class
Schools and NMMS close 15-11-2026, and states differ a lot (Delhi 15-02-2027, Haryana 31-01-2027, Punjab SC
15-11-2026, Assam and Himachal already closed). Always show the date of THAT row plus "checked on 05 Nov 2026
on scholarships.gov.in", and compute open/closed/not-yet-open against today (passed in, in IST), never hardcoded.

Every other row of nsp-data.js (state schemes, Himachal, Manipur, Puducherry...) is listed for that state with its date and guideline link and the words "rules not read by us, open the guideline"; never a ceiling.

No minority scheme: the three NSP lists of 2026-27 show no post-matric or merit-cum-means scheme of the Ministry of Minority Affairs (only one Uttarakhand pre-matric state scheme). Say exactly that for a minority student ("as listed on 05 Nov 2026"), do not explain why, and still show what fits their other answers.

Where to apply (`portalFor`): the ST guideline (para 5.i) says each state builds its own platform or opts for NSP. So: if the state has a row in nsp-data.js for that category, the answer is NSP with that row. If not, the answer is the state's portal, marked "NSP does not list this scheme for your state; the state runs it; dates and rules NOT verified by us, the state may use a different income limit" (real example: Madhya Pradesh's portal shows a different OBC limit, Karnataka too). Show the central ceiling only as "central guideline", and do NOT filter out by income there. Portals (official domain seen on 05/11; most Indian state sites refuse connections from outside India, so say in the README that we could not open UP, BR, CG, MP, RJ, KA, TS, AP, WB, GJ, JH, TN from our machine):
UP https://scholarship.up.gov.in · BR (SC/ST) https://scstpmsonline.bihar.gov.in/scstpms2223/pms/StudentDefault.aspx · CG https://postmatric-scholarship.cg.nic.in · MP https://hescholarship.mp.gov.in · RJ https://sjmsnew.rajasthan.gov.in/scholarship/ · KA https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in · TS https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in · AP https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in · WB https://oasis.wb.gov.in · GJ https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/CitizenNew/ScholarshipServices.aspx · JH https://ekalyan.cgg.gov.in · KL https://egrantz.kerala.gov.in · OD https://scholarship.odisha.gov.in · MH https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in · TN SC/ST https://tnadw.tn.gov.in/scholarshipform/en and BC https://bcw.tn.gov.in/sub_page/8. Any other state with no row and no portal here: "not verified", link to the NSP scheme-wise nodal officers page https://scholarships.gov.in/nodalofficerList (the helpdesk below also works). Never guess a portal.

Applying more than one: "from AY 2026-27 one merit-based scheme plus one or more welfare-based schemes" (F/NSP_FAQsORApplicationUsermanul_V2.2.pdf Q3 and NSP home). But several guidelines allow holding only one scholarship: say both.

Before applying (OTR, F/OTR%20FAQ%20v1.4.pdf Q5): One Time Registration is mandatory; active mobile, Aadhaar number, mobile linked with Aadhaar; without Aadhaar, the enrolment slip or the parent's or guardian's Aadhaar if a minor, and an EID-based OTR must add Aadhaar within one month or it may be suspended (Q27). NSP OTR app on Google Play plus AadhaarFaceRD; error 904 means update that app; biometrics locked in Aadhaar make face authentication fail (NSP home notices). At a CSC the whole thing costs Rs 30 all-inclusive (NSP home notice; locator https://locator.csccloud.in).

Documents (quote per scheme): CSSS fresh: Class XII mark sheet, family income certificate, caste certificate if reserved, disability certificate if applicable; renewal: previous year mark sheet (G/FAQ_DOHE_CSSS.pdf). Post-matric SC and OBC/EBC/DNT: passport photo, certificates of all exams passed, caste certificate signed by the authorised officer, income certificate from the competent authority set by the state (SC para 8.3 in manipur_3032_G, YASASVI para 8.3). Disability: photo, income certificate signed by revenue authority or tehsildar (Form 16 not accepted), Aadhaar, disability certificate, last mark sheet, fee receipt, passbook or cancelled cheque; PDF or JPEG up to 200 KB; below Rs 50,000 nothing is uploaded and the papers go to the institute (DEPDFAQ Q13). Income certificate once per course (SC 5.3, ST 3.3). Others: "see the guideline" link, do not invent.

The 3 checks and what stops the money:
1. Aadhaar-seeded bank account. Money goes to the Aadhaar-seeded account (G/FAQ_DOHE_CSSS.pdf Q6-9); check at npci.org.in, Consumer tab, Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler (BASE), "Get Aadhaar Mapped Status" (G/npci_base.pdf); if the OTR says "Not seeded" though it is, use "Aadhaar Seeding, Check it now" in OTR Profile (OTR FAQ Q20). Account active, new IFSC if the bank merged, no credit limit (DEPDFAQ Q23; F/NSP_SOP.pdf 3a). PFMS rejects bad bank details (DEPDFAQ Q18) and NSP allows ONE chance to update them (SOP 3k).
2. Same name everywhere. Name, father's name and roll number exactly as on the mark sheet: example in the FAQ, "Shri Mahendra Pratap Singh" on the mark sheet and "M.P. Singh" on Aadhaar means the scheme will not be visible (CSSS FAQ Q15). Fix before submitting: after submission, OTR changes need withdrawing and a fresh application while the scheme is open (NSP FAQ Q12, Q13); a fresh application cannot be revoked once withdrawn (Q6). 
3. Institute verification. Follow up with your institute before ITS deadline (the institute date of that row); act on NSP SMS; correct a Defective mark by the defect date; picking the wrong institute means it has to mark it defective first (SOP 3e-3i; NSP FAQ Q8).
After approval: track on PFMS "Know Your Payment" with the application ID https://pfms.nic.in/SitePages/KnowYourPayment_Dw_NewNew.aspx; if PFMS says Success, check the account that was seeded at that moment (CSSS FAQ Q6). Status https://scholarships.gov.in/payl/loginPage.action. Help: NSP helpdesk 0120-6619540, PFMS toll free 1800 118 111, NSP eligibility check https://scholarships.gov.in/scholarshipEligibility, grievance https://nsp.gov.in/grievanceregistration/.

Scale and severity (README and submission only): PMS-SC: Cabinet approved Rs 59,048 crore for more than 4 crore SC students over 5 years (PIB PRID 1682967, 23 Dec 2020); nearly 48 lakh SC students a year, 48,04,208 beneficiaries and Rs 5,562.23 crore of central assistance in 2024-25 (PIB PRID 2287841, 22 Jul 2026). Retention at secondary level 51.9% in 2025-26, dropout 7.0% (UDISE+ 2025-26, PIB PRID 2282141). Do not claim a dropout-to-money causal number: none was found.

## Build (same pattern as Ghar Ki Hawa, Nyaya Sahayak and Fasal Nuksan Sahayak in ~/Backups/impressora)

- Static web app (index.html + app.js + i18n en/hi), no framework, < 100 KB, works on 3G; one
  Cloudflare Worker for the anonymous counter.
- Flow, one step per screen: course now (Class 11-12, ITI/diploma, UG, PG, PhD) -> state of
  domicile -> category (SC, ST, OBC, EBC, DNT, minority community, person with disability,
  general; more than one allowed) -> annual family income (bands that match the official
  ceilings) -> fresh or renewal -> girl? technical AICTE course? any Swanath situation? disability 40%+? passed Class XII in the North East? (short optional step). No marks question: on renewal of CSSS, ask 50% marks and 75% attendance.
- Result card: the schemes that fit, each with the rule quoted and its source; WHERE to apply
  (NSP or the named state portal, official link); the deadline with the date it was checked;
  the documents list; the 3 checks before submitting (Aadhaar-seeded bank account and how to check
  it, names matching across documents, institute verification); "Copy checklist" and a WhatsApp
  share; a "Did you apply?" yes/no box for the counter.
- rules.js = pure functions (eligibleSchemes, portalFor, deadlineFor, documentsFor) with
  `node --test` tests: each income ceiling edge (ceiling inside, ceiling + 1 outside), a category
  with no scheme, a state with no verified data (must answer "not verified, use the state
  portal"), the text never promising money, Hindi and English keys complete. See each test fail
  once against a broken rule before trusting it (mutation check, like Ghar Ki Hawa).
- `?nostats` disables every event (as app.js line 15-18 of ~/Backups/impressora/ghar-ki-hawa). Worker, wrangler.toml (name chhatravritti-sahayak, same account_id), .assetsignore (also tools) and stats.html: copy the shape from ../ghar-ki-hawa, without forecast, cron or readings. Events: check, schemes (count only), copy, share, applied_yes, applied_no.
- /stats page and /api/stats JSON like the others: checks, schemes shown, checklists copied or
  shared, "Did you apply?" yes/no, by language and day; no personal data. This is the Proven
  impact evidence.
- README: setup, sources for every rule, ceiling and date, how AgentFoundry was used, MIT license.
