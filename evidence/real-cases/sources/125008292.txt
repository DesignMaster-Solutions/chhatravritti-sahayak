1. Heard.

2. With the consent of Sri Vinod Kumar Singh, learned counsel for the petitioner and learned Standing Counsel appearing for the State, the present petition is being decided at the admission stage itself.

3. Present petition has been preferred for quashing of the impugned order dated 26.02.2024 passed by the respondent no. 2 i.e. Director, Social Welfare, Lucknow whereby rejected the claim of the petitioner for payment of scholarship for B.A.M.S. (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) 4th year i.e. Academic Session 2020-21 and with a further prayer to direct the respondents to sanction the scholarship to the petitioner for Academic Session 2020-21 within the time stipulated by this Court.

4. Learned Counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the petitioner has got admission in B.A.M.S. four year course in the year 2017 in G.S. Ayurvedic Medical College and Hospital, Pilkhuwan, City and District Hapur. The petitioner belongs to scheduled caste category and in pursuance of the relevant government order, the petitioner was entitled for sanction of post metric scholarship awarded by the Social Welfare Department in favour of those students who are poor and belonging to Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribes category.

5. It is further submitted that the petitioner being fully eligible, the Social Welfare Department sanctioned and granted scholarship in favour of the petitioner for the academic sessions 2017-18, 2018-19 and 2019-20 but no scholarship was given to the petitioner for the fourth i.e. final year (Academic Session 2020-21).

6. It is further submitted that against the non sanction of the scholarship for the academic session 2020-21, the father of the petitioner moved an application before the respondent no. 2 and 3. The District Social Welfare Officer had written a letter dated 12.07.2021 recommending for sanction of scholarship in favour of the petitioner (copy of which is enclosed as annexure no. 12 of the writ petition).

7. Despite recommendation and clarifying the position in favour of the petitioner by the letter of District Social Welfare Officer dated 12.07.2021, no orders were passed by the respondent no. 2. Thereafter representations were made by the father of the petitioner to the respondent no. 2.

8. It is further submitted that the Director, Social Welfare Department, asked the Joint Director, Social Welfare Department to submit a report and in pursuance thereof on 03.02.2024, a report was submitted by the Joint Director, Social Welfare Department categorically mentioning therein that the petitioner had submitted her form within time. But the respondent no. 2 by passing the impugned order dated 26.02.2024 rejected the claim of the petitioner for grant of scholarship for the academic session 2020-21 on the ground that the institution had not forwarded the documents.

9. It is further submitted that perusal of report submitted by the Joint Director, Social Welfare Department and the impugned order show that there is no fault on the part of the petitioner and due to the fault on the part of the institution, petitioner could not make sufferer.

10. On the other hand, learned Standing Counsel has submitted that the scholarship could only be sanctioned or granted to those eligible students whose papers are forwarded by the institution within the time prescribed and in the present case, the department had not received scholarship form from the institution within time prescribed, so there is no illegality in the order dated 26.02.2024 passed by the respondent no. 2.

11. After hearing learned counsel for the parties, going through the record of the case and the report dated 03.02.2024 submitted by the Joint Director, Social Welfare Department in compliance of the directions of Director, Social Welfare Department, the position which emerges out in the present case is that the petitioner has submitted her application alongwith relevant documents within time prescribed and if the same were not forwarded by the institution for that, the petitioner could not make sufferer as otherwise the petitioner is eligible for grant of scholarship.

12. The report of Joint Director, Social Welfare Department dated 03.02.2024 also reveals that at the time of inquiry on 02.02.2024, the institution had informed that during that period, the form was not shown on the website/portal so at that juncture, the documents were unable to be uploaded but the application of the petitioner and other documents in hard copy were sent to the office of respondent no. 3-Social Welfare Department. The impugned order dated 26.02.2024 does not speak even a single word that the petitioner is not eligible as per the government orders issued time to time for the purpose of grant of scholarship, it has been rejected only due to the fault of the institution for which the petitioner cannot be made sufferer.

13. In view of the aforesaid facts, circumstances and discussion made hereinabove, the writ petition is allowed.

14. The impugned order dated 26.02.2024 passed by the respondent no. 2-Director, Social Welfare Department, Lucknow is hereby quashed.

15. Respondent no. 2 is directed to ensure the payment of scholarship to the petitioner for the academic session 2020-21 within a period of six weeks from the date of production of certified copy of this order.
