Information sought

:

1. The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 24/06/2025 before the CPIO, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, New Delhi seeking the following information:
"I am writing this RTI application under the Right to Information Act, 2005, to seek specific information regarding the delay and non-payment of the 2nd installment of my Post Matric Scholarship. My scholarship details are as follows: Applicant Name: Ashant Vishnuji Dudhe Application ID: 2223********55693 Applied Date: 09/11/2022 2nd Installment Approved on: 30/11/2023 1st Installment Credited on: 01/08/2024 Institute Name: St. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering and Technology, Nagpur I have not yet received my 2nd installment, even though it has been approved. This delay is causing me serious academic and financial inconvenience. Kindly provide the following information:
1) What is the current status of my 2nd installment disbursement?
2) Why has it not been credited yet, despite the approval on 30/11/2023?
3) Which officer/department is responsible for processing or delaying this payment?
4) When can I expect the pending amount to be credited to my account?..."

2. The CPIO has furnished a reply to the appellant dated 01/09/2025 stated as under:

"Centrally sponsored Schemes of Post Matric Scholarship Scheme for SC Students is open-ended scheme and Central share of scholarship amount is disbursed directly into the Aadhaar seeded bank accounts of the beneficiaries through DBT after the States/UTs release their share of scholarship amount and transmit data to Central Portal. The scheme is implemented through State Government/UT Administration."

3. Aggrieved and dissatisfied, the appellant filed the First Appeal with the first Appellate Authority. The First Appellate Authority vide order dated 01/09/2025 stated as under:

"The matter has been examined and it is observed that CPIO has already provided the available information as sought by the applicant In view of the above facts, the first appeal stands disposed of."

4. Being aggrieved, the Appellant filed the Instant Second Appeal on 03/09/2025.

The Appellant vide email dated 30/07/2026 withdrew his appeal stating, "Subject: Request to Close the Appeal as the Matter Has Been Resolved Respected Sir/Madam, With reference to the hearing scheduled on 04 August 2026 regarding my appeal related to the Government of India Post Matric Scholarship, I would like to inform you that I have now received the pending scholarship amount.

Since the matter has been resolved and the relief sought has already been provided, I do not wish to pursue the appeal further. Therefore, I respectfully request that the scheduled hearing may kindly be treated as unnecessary and the appeal may be disposed of accordingly.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Yours faithfully, Ashant Vishnuji Dudhe"

Written Submission of the Respondent is taken on record.

5. Relevant Facts emerged during Hearing:

The following were present:-
Appellant: Absent Respondent: Ms. Deepshika, Assistant Director participated in the hearing in Person

6. The Appellant has not availed the opportunity to participate in the hearing despite due notice of hearing.

7. The Respondent apprises the bench that the appellant withdrew his case.

DECISION

8. The Commission notes that the Appellant vide email dated 30/07/2026 withdrew his case. Hence the case stands closed as withdrawn. The Appeal stands disposed of.
