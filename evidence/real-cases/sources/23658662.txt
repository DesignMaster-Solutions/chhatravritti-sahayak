Hon'ble Alok Mathur,J.
 


Heard Ms. Ranjana Agnihotri, learned counsel for the petitioner as well as learned Standing Counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents.

The petitioner by means of the instant writ petition is espousing the cause of 232 students who belonged to the scheduled caste category and were entitled for getting the benefit of 'Dasmottar Chhatravriti Yojana' of the State Government, whereby they are entitled to receive compensation towards the non refundable fees paid by them for the educational courses as stated in the scheme.

It has been submitted that out of 449 students belonging to Scheduled Caste category the State Government had made payment to only 217 students, while the remaining 232 students have been denied benefit of the said scheme on one reason or the other. It has been submitted that 232 students belonged to the scheduled caste category studying in the petitioner's institution are entitled to benefit of the said scholarship scheme and the State Government in as most illegal arbitrary manner denied the benefit of the same and therefore a direction has been sought to the State Government to disburse the amount of scholarship to the remaining 232 students.

It has further been submitted that the reason for denying of the Scholarship has been mentioned as " rejected by District Scholarship Committee due to flaw (Caste/Income/Domicile Certificate or Income Amount or High school Roll Number or Account Holder Name not matched or suspected fee details)".

A counter affidavit ha been filed filed by the Deputy Director Pramod Kumar Tripathi has been stated that the Central share not been received by the State Government to the tune of Rs.1088.6024 Crores as is evident from the letter of the Chief Secretary Government of U.P. dated 29.07.2015. Due to this financial crunch the Scholarship fees has not been distributed amongst the students who are eligible for the same.

The counsel for the petitioner has also annexed a copy of the scheme which was notified on 20.09.2014, according to which it has clear that the scheme has been promulgated by the State of U.P. for the benefit of the students belonging to the scheduled caste category who are studying in the institutions which are recognized by the State of U.P. and also in the course which is recognized.

From perusal of the scheme it is no where mentioned that the disbursement of scholarship would be subject to any grant from the Central Government. The counter affidavit in this regard does not seem to be quite correct, inasmuch as it has been stated that petitioners are entitled to some Scheduled Caste Post Matriculation Scheme, while the petitioner in the writ petition has claimed the scholarship under the Dasmottar Chhatravriti Yojana.

It has further brought to the notice of this Court that according to the said scheme in Clause 17(iv) any student who has been denied the scholarship on account of rejection of some of the papers or certificate is entitled to prefer an appeal to the District Magistrate and the District Magistrate shall consider and decide the same by a reasoned and speaking order.

Learned counsel for the petitioner appearing on behalf of the institution has made a statement that none of the students have preferred an appeal as provided for in the scheme and therefore has confined her prayer to the extent that District Magistrate be directed to consider the grievance of the students individually and pass a reasoned and speaking order with regard to the entitlement of scholarship under the Dasmottar Chhatravriti Yojana.

I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the record.

The perusal of the aforesaid scheme under Clause 17(iv) provides that in case any student is denied the benefit of Scholarship under the said scheme, he is entitled to move an appeal before the District Magistrate, who shall consider the case of the students and pass appropriate orders considering the grievance raised by the students therein.

In light of the above, the petition is disposed of with liberty to the students to prefer an appeal before the District Magistrate. In case such an appeal is made to the District Magistrate, he will consider the same and decide it by a reasoned and speaking order within two months from the date of production of a certified copy of this order, in accordance with law.

With the aforesaid direction the writ petition stands disposed of.
