Information sought

:

1. The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 30/08/2025 before the CPIO, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, New Delhi seeking the following information:
"Kindly provide the following information related to my NSP scholarship application:
1. What is the prescribed time limit for each stage of application processing (Institute verification - State verification - Ministry approval - Payment generation) under the concerned scholarship scheme?
2. Has my application ID BR202***********4659 (Applicant Name: Raju Kumar, Son of Sudama Yadav, Category: OBC, College: NIT Nagaland) been approved for payment. If yes, please provide date of approval and payment details.
3. If my application is still pending, please specify the exact stage where it is pending and the reason for delay.
4. Provide the name and designation of the officer responsible for processing applications at State level and Ministry level for OBC category scholarships."

2. The CPIO has furnished a reply to the appellant dated 04/09/2025 stated as under:

"Your application is not included in the merit list as per the annexure provided by the institute. For further information, you are advised to contact your Institute Nodal Officer. Concerned Officer at Ministry level is Vicky Vikas Sinha."

3. Aggrieved and dissatisfied, the appellant filed the First Appeal with the first Appellate Authority. The First Appellate Authority vide order dated 12/09/2025 stated as under:

"Your application has not been included in the merit list as per the annexure provided by the institute. The NIT Nagaland has been allotted 21 slots while the institute verified 62 beneficiaries which exceeds the allotted slots. Accordingly the ministry has disbursed scholarships only for 21 slots strictly based on the annexure submitted by the institute. For further details or clarification, you are advised to contact your Institute Nodal Officer."

4. Being aggrieved, the Appellant filed the Instant Second Appeal on 18/09/2025.

Written Submission of the Appellant is taken on record.

The Appellant vide letter dated 02/07/2026 withdrew his Second Appeal stating, "Respected Sir, I, Raju Kumar, appellant in the aforementioned file, had filed a second appeal regarding an RTI application dated 30-08-2025 addressed to the CPIO, ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment.

I am glad to inform the Commission that I have now successfully received my scholarship from the concerned ministry. Since my grievance has been completely resolved and I am fully Satisfied, I no longer wish to peruse this matter.

Therefore, I respectfully request the Hon'ble Commission to accept this withdrawal application, Cancel the hearing Scheduled for 16-07-2026 at 11:00AM and close the Case file."

5. Relevant Facts emerged during Hearing:

The following were present:-
Appellant: Absent Respondent: Absent

6. Neither the Appellant nor the Respondent has not availed the opportunity to participate in the hearing despite due notice of hearing.

DECISION

7. The Commission notes that the Appellant vide letter dated 02/07/2026 withdrew his Second Appeal. Hence the case stands closed as withdrawn. The Appeal stands disposed of.
